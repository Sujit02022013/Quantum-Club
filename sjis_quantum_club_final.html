01 · About the club

A place to turn curiosity into physics.

The SJIS Quantum Club is presented as a learning community where students can question how nature works at the smallest and largest scales—from particles and probability to stars, black holes and the structure of the universe.

Through talks, demonstrations, problem-solving, projects, observations and collaborative activities, members can build scientific intuition while developing communication, research and experimental thinking.

05 · Quantum journey

From the first quantum idea to the next generation.

Use this timeline as a visual starting point for club talks and student research topics.

1900

Planck and the quantum hypothesis

Energy enters physics through discrete packets in a breakthrough that reshaped classical thinking.

1905

Einstein and light quanta

The photoelectric effect helped establish a deeper link between light and quantization.

1920s

Wave mechanics and a new language

Schrödinger, Heisenberg and others developed mathematical frameworks for quantum phenomena.

Today

Quantum information & frontier science

Quantum technologies connect physics with computing, sensing, communication and research.
